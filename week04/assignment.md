**Due:** Before Week 5 session  
**Submit:** One `.R` script + written section (200–300 words total)

## Instructions

Use the dataset you found in Week 3 (or choose a new one). Identify at least **one categorical variable** and **one continuous variable**.

> 💡 The written section from this assignment will become the descriptive statistics section of your final project. Save it.

---

### Task 1: Describe a categorical variable

- Create a bar plot using ggplot2
- Compute counts per category using `group_by()` + `summarize()`
- Write 1–2 sentences describing what you observe — using **sample-appropriate language**

### Task 2: Describe a continuous variable

- Create a histogram using ggplot2
- Assess the distribution shape (normal or skewed?)
- Report the appropriate summary statistics (mean + SD, or five-number summary)
- Write 1–2 sentences describing what you observe

### Task 3: Relationship between two variables

- Choose any two variables and create a plot showing their relationship (scatter plot, grouped bar plot, faceted histogram, etc.)
- Write 1 paragraph describing the relationship — staying within descriptive language ("in our dataset / sample")

---

## Sample-appropriate language

✅ "In our dataset, Adelie penguins have a mean bill length of 38.8mm."  
❌ "Adelie penguins have shorter bills than Chinstrap penguins." (requires inferential statistics)
