# Final Project — Introduction to R and Statistics for PROPEL

**Groups:** 3–4 students  
**Presentations:** Week 8

---

## Overview

In this project, you will apply data manipulation and statistics to analyze a dataset of your choosing and present your findings in a short video.

Work on this project using the skills you have learned in the class up to Week 7. Before the Week 8 session, complete the [Week 7 checklist](../week07/assignment.md) to make sure you’re ready.

---

## Step 1: Select a dataset

Choose any publicly available dataset. You may use sources including:

- [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets)
- [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/)
- [data.gov](https://data.gov/)
- [Dataset ideas from 2023 PROPEL cohort](https://ucsf.box.com/s/xj4j554yx9belxmdswdocatqt1gmb9ri)

> ⚠️ Do **not** use datasets containing protected health information (PHI) or nonpublic data belonging to a research group.

---

## Step 2: Apply descriptive statistics

Use the data manipulation and descriptive statistics skills from Weeks 3–4. In addition to visualizations covered in class, you may explore the [R Graph Gallery](https://r-graph-gallery.com/) for ideas.

---

## Step 3: Apply inferential statistics

Apply confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, regression, or survival analysis to investigate a question about your dataset. You may use techniques not covered in class.

---

## Step 4: Create a 2-minute video

Record a screen-capture video covering:

- **Introduction:** Briefly introduce your group.
- **Dataset:** Why and how was it collected? What does it include, and what are its limitations?
- **Descriptive insight:** What technique did you apply? What did you find? Include a visualization.
- **Inferential insight:** What technique did you apply? How do you interpret the results? Include a visualization.

**Maximum duration: 2 minutes.** This is a real constraint — practice and edit.

Recommended: [Zoom Record](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059856) or [Zoom Clips](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0057723)

## AI use policy

You may use any AI tool (e.g., UCSF ChatGPT Enterprise, Google Gemini) for this project. If you do:

- Save all prompts you used in a file called `prompts.txt` (ideally in your RStudio project folder)
- Include this file in your GitHub repository
- Add a note in the "Notes" column of the submission table indicating that AI tools were used

> **Recommendation:** Actively type code rather than copy-pasting from AI. You will learn more and understand your own analysis better.
